# Road to Football Agent

A self-study course for the FIFA Football Agent Exam, plus a practical track on doing the job.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies.

## What is in it

- **Route** – exam format, the yearly licensing cycle, a 12-week study plan, progress.
- **Exam course** – 14 lessons covering the FFAR, RSTP, Statutes, Football Tribunal, Disciplinary Code, Code of Ethics and safeguarding material.
- **Practice** – 65 original questions with explanations. Topic drills and a timed 20-question mock marked the way FIFA marks (all-or-nothing per question, 75% to pass).
- **The trade** – 19 lessons in three practical sections (recruiting players, dealing with clubs, going global) plus business set-up, with case studies, copyable templates and checklists.
- **Sources** – official FIFA documents, free third-party mocks, and the commentary used.

## Files

- `index.html` – all lesson content.
- `assets/questions.js` – the question bank. Add a question by appending an object (`t` topic, `q`, `o` options, `a` indices of every correct option, `e` explanation, `r` reference).
- `assets/app.js` – navigation, progress (saved in `localStorage`), drill and mock exam.
- `assets/style.css` – styles, light and dark.

## Caveats

Unofficial. Not affiliated with FIFA or the John Viola Academy. FIFA publishes no past papers; the questions here are original. Parts of the FFAR are suspended (Circular 1873) and a revised RSTP takes effect on 1 January 2027, so check everything against the current FIFA Study Materials.
